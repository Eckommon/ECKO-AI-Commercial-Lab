# NOVAEL ARC v1.3 — Commercial Rationale

Status: `SOL_CREATIVE_REMEDIATION_AUTHORIZED / IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_AUTHORIZED`
Verdict: `COMMERCIAL_DESIGN_PASS` pending independent GPT review

This is the design-only response to the human playback failure recorded for v1.2. It does not authorize production, modify governed v1.2 inputs, claim that missing motion sources exist, or establish media acceptance.

## Commercial proposition

**NOVAEL ARC turns ambient light into a sculptural focal point worth pausing for.**

The target viewer is a design-conscious adult attracted to restrained interiors and expressive objects. The viewer should understand by 3.2 seconds that this is an advertisement for a named sculptural lamp, feel a material-to-light transformation at the center, and remember five seconds later: the crescent form, `NOVAEL ARC`, `LIGHT, HELD AS FORM.`, and the three-note rising mnemonic.

## Why v1.2 failed commercially

v1.2 treated five static plates as the finished editorial vocabulary. Seven long beats, six default hard cuts, two slight scale changes, a five-second complete freeze, generic one-word copy, late/no explicit product naming, and a low-density bed produced technical composure without advertising energy. The lamp remained visible, but visibility did not become association, desire or recall. Audio confirmed cues existed; it did not create an identity.

v1.3 preserves the engineering baseline but rejects the assumption that code can manufacture cinematography from flattened stills. Four purpose-shot motion plates are mandatory. The current stills become brief recognition, proposition, release and closing anchors rather than the substance of the entire film.

## Audience attention strategy and hook

The first 1.5 seconds use a genuine macro motion plate: a warm edge appears against black, the camera accelerates along the inner curve, and a graphic arc trace catches up to it. A tactile stone click, reverse-air pull and the first two notes of the mnemonic make the first frame feel intentional. At 1.5 seconds the trace wipes to the intact hero, the full lamp becomes recognizable, and the final mnemonic note lands. `NOVAEL ARC` is complete by 2.25 seconds and remains held through 4.0 seconds.

This solves three opening problems together: motion earns attention, the whole product resolves early enough for visibility, and naming binds the unusual silhouette to a recallable identity.

## Visual mnemonic

The visual mnemonic is a **warm arc trace** that follows the product's real crescent while remaining outside the product silhouette whenever it moves directionally. It has only four appearances:

1. reveal edge in the hook;
2. wipe into whole-product recognition;
3. exterior echo at the light-event arrival;
4. final draw toward the product tip before the brand lockup.

It is always a clearly editorial graphic outside or at the boundary of the photographed object. Base-to-tip direction belongs exclusively to this exterior trace. The photographed diffuser instead rises globally and spatially coherently, with stable topology and relative illumination pattern. The graphic never masquerades as a controllable chase light, emitter animation or technical product capability. Its repetition links form, motion, transformation and name without introducing a factual claim.

## Product hierarchy

1. complete crescent silhouette and base;
2. explicit `NOVAEL ARC` name;
3. frosted inner arc and amber transformation;
4. `LIGHT, HELD AS FORM.` positioning line;
5. `HOLD THE LIGHT.` campaign line;
6. stone, graphite texture and negative space.

The complete product is legible by 1.5 seconds and named by 2.25 seconds. Material detail follows recognition rather than replacing it. The light event does not arrive until the viewer knows what object is transforming. The close holds product, name and campaign line together for 2.8 seconds.

| Beat | Time | Function | Primary movement | Copy |
|---|---:|---|---|---|

The required functions are explicit: S01 is the 0–2 second hook; S02 is product recognition; S03–S04 seduce through material and form; S05 is the hero light event; S06 is controlled release; S07–S09 build and hold brand memory.

## Motion grammar

Motion alternates between genuine camera/source movement, short source-safe still treatment and settled recognition. No still is asked to simulate an orbit, separated depth or physical activation.

- Genuine motion: S01, S03, S05 and S07 use mandatory new motion plates.
- Source-safe still movement: S02, S04, S06 and S08 use 1–2.5 percent whole-plate pushes or pulls that settle quickly.
- Graphic movement: the arc trace/wipe and kinetic type create editorial rhythm without changing product physics.
- Stillness: limited to resolved holds. The longest complete still is the 2.8-second final memory hold; S06 has no more than 1.3 seconds of total stillness after deceleration.

This creates a pulse of hook → recognize → inspect → name the idea → transform → release → desire → state → remember.

## Transition grammar

Hard cuts are used for impact at S03, S06 and S07, not as the only safe default. Arc-shaped wipes connect S01→S02, S03→S04, S07→S08 and S08→S09. A two-frame luminance dip before S05 creates anticipation but is explicitly editorial. Every wipe follows the familiar crescent and terminates quickly enough that it cannot become decorative wallpaper.

Cross-dissolves between unlike stills remain prohibited because they double geometry. Motion-plate endpoints must be framed to match their following anchors; if they do not match, the plate is rejected rather than repaired by a long dissolve.

## Typography and complete copy set

The complete v1.3 copy set is:

- `NOVAEL ARC`
- `LIGHT, HELD AS FORM.`
- `HOLD THE LIGHT.`

All three are non-technical, non-performance and non-comparative. `NOVAEL ARC` is product identification; `LIGHT, HELD AS FORM.` is emotional positioning; `HOLD THE LIGHT.` remains the governed campaign line. `FORM.` and `GLOW.` are retired because isolated generic nouns did not create enough association.

Use a bold modern grotesk for the product name and medium weights for the two statements. At 1080×1920 the product-name cap height is at least 92 px and secondary copy at least 58 px. Every full-copy state must be checked at 360×640. Kinetic type resolves quickly into fixed, fully readable words; no line depends on blur, microtype or letter-by-letter decoding.

## Association and desire strategy

Association comes from repetition with purpose, not added claims. The same crescent appears as edge, complete object, material path, light path and graphic arc. The proposition names the emotional idea after the viewer has inspected the material. The transformation then rewards that setup. Repeating the name on S07 and the close prevents the campaign line from floating free of the product.

Desire comes from tactile proximity, real movement and contrast: a fast hook, controlled macro detail, a central warm transformation, a quiet release, and a final camera glide. The lamp is not presented as technology. It is presented as an object whose form organizes the light around it.

## Audio arc and sonic mnemonic

The sonic mnemonic is a tactile click plus a three-note warm rising figure. It is stated in fragments and resolved three times:

- S01–S02: hook and product recognition;
- S04–S05: proposition pulses grow into the complete transformation figure;
- S08–S09: two setup notes resolve as the final glass-and-stone brand sting.

The tonal bed is supporting tissue, not the concept. S03 adds two tactile material accents. S05 widens harmonically and provides the strongest arrival. S06 falls into a deliberate 350 ms near-silent pocket with audible room tone. S07 returns with a more present pulse. The endpoint reaches digital silence by 29.92 seconds without an overrun.

Muted playback should feel materially weaker because sound supplies the hook's pull, the material scale, the light-event anticipation, the release contrast and the final recall pattern. The audio does not merely confirm cuts and must remain meaningful in mono on phone speakers.

## End-card and brand-memory strategy

S08 states `HOLD THE LIGHT.` before the end card so the final frame is not forced to introduce every idea. S09 cuts to the stable end-card plate at 26.5 seconds. The arc trace finishes by 27.35; `NOVAEL ARC` is primary, `HOLD THE LIGHT.` is secondary, and both remain complete from 27.2 through 30.0 seconds. The final mnemonic note lands when the name locks. There is no fade to black or outgoing type animation.

## Why this no longer feels like a slideshow

- Four mandatory motion plates supply real macro, light and whole-object camera movement.
- Still assets are short anchors with settled pushes, not five-second displays.
- The visual arc mnemonic crosses beats and motivates transitions.
- Kinetic type provides rhythm while preserving readable holds.
- Energy changes across nine unequal beats rather than seven similarly weighted stills.
- The audio has an identifiable mnemonic, tactile material events, a center peak, contrast pocket and final sting.
- Missing motion plates fail closed; production may not substitute long still pans or brightness tweens.

## Reusable Factory capability vs NOVAEL treatment

Potentially reusable Factory capability includes timeline-driven unequal beats, curve-based wipe primitives, settled push/pull camera moves, grouped kinetic-type cues, source-availability gates, endpoint-matching metadata, cue synchronization, mono/phone audio tests and final-copy hold validation.

NOVAEL-specific treatment includes the crescent path, graphite/amber palette, exact copy, three-note mnemonic pitches/timbres, plate prompts, geometry acceptance reference, arc-trace placements, light-event timing and end-card composition. None of those belongs in generic Factory defaults.

## Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | Required response |
|---|---|
| Mandatory motion plate is missing | Fail closed before implementation; do not stretch a still. |
| Motion plate changes geometry/material identity | Reject and regenerate against `shot-01-hero.png`; do not repair it in code. |
| The hero event suggests an internal sequential activation or chase-light feature | Require a global, spatially coherent illumination increase with stable diffuser topology and relative pattern; reserve all directional base-to-tip movement for the exterior graphic trace. |
| Kinetic copy is unreadable at 360×640 | Collapse to whole-phrase reveals at identical timings while preserving fixed holds. |
| Still/motion endpoint mismatch | Regenerate or reframe the motion plate; do not hide mismatch with a long cross-dissolve. |
| Audio disappears on phones or in mono | Rebalance from mono-first monitoring, simplify the bed, and preserve mnemonic notes/transients. |
| Baked source band is objectionable | Use a contained intact source or request replacement; no cleanup of canonical bytes. |
